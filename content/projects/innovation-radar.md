## Overview

The **Innovation Radar** is an institutional web platform engineered for the **GoTec** research group at IFRO to index, track, and showcase technological innovation projects, intellectual property, and research outputs across educational hubs.

---

## Key Contributions

- **Frontend Component Library:** Designed and developed reusable UI components in **TypeScript & ReactJS**, cataloged and tested using **Storybook** for visual isolation.
- **RESTful API Backend:** Implemented backend data endpoints in **Laravel (PHP)** with **MySQL**, supporting role-based access control (RBAC), project approval lifecycles, and relational metrics.
- **Scientific Dissemination:** Presented the architecture and social impact at major regional scientific gatherings including **Expo Favela** and the **National Week of Science and Technology (SNCT)**.
- **Dockerized Team Pipeline:** Created containerized multi-container setups to ensure deterministic testing and onboarding across junior student researchers.
